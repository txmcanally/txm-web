Ring Blast 3D

A cylindrical falling-block puzzle for iPhone — Android coming soon. Rotate the board, clear complete rings, and climb the high-score boards.

Support
iPhone now · Android soon

Gameplay

Falling blocks on a spinning cylinder.

Drag to rotate the cylinder, tap to spin the piece, and clear complete rings before the stack hits the top. Big multi-ring clears drop bombs — and every level turns up the speed.

  • Drag to rotate the cylinder; tap to spin pieces.
  • Clear full rings around the 3D board.
  • Bombs unlock after big multi-ring clears.
  • Game Center leaderboards for high scores.
  • Built for short sessions and repeat play.
