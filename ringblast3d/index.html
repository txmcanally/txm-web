Classic puzzle pressure, wrapped around a cylinder.

Ring Blast 3D keeps the satisfying shape-reading rhythm of falling-block puzzles, then bends the board into a rotating 3D ring. Drag to rotate the cylinder, tap to spin pieces, and clear complete rings before the stack reaches the top.

  • Rotating cylindrical board
  • Fast tap and drag controls
  • Bomb blocks for multi-ring clears
  • Rising speed and high-score chasing
  • Game Center leaderboard support
