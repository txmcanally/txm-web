Ring Blast 3D

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 31, 2026

TXMcanally, LLC built Ring Blast 3D as a puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is used.

Information We Collect

Ring Blast 3D does not collect personal information directly from you. The app does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs.

The app stores gameplay preferences and progress locally on your device, such as high score, sound settings, music settings, and tutorial completion. This information stays on your device and is not sent to TXMcanally, LLC.

Game Center

Ring Blast 3D uses Apple Game Center for leaderboard functionality. If you choose to use Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center profile, player identifier, leaderboard scores, and related Game Center information according to Apple's privacy policy and Game Center terms. Ring Blast 3D submits your score to Game Center only for leaderboard functionality.

Data Sharing

TXMcanally, LLC does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Children's Privacy

Ring Blast 3D is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local gameplay data remains on your device until you delete the app or reset the data through device or app settings where available. Game Center data is managed by Apple through your Apple Account and Game Center settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact TXMcanally, LLC at support@txm-games.com.
